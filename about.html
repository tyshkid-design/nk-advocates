About

Our Firm

N Kuria Advocates are advocates, commissioners for oaths and notaries public based in Nairobi, advising clients in Kenya and beyond.

Our values

Honesty. Integrity. Commitment.

Honesty

We give you straight advice, even when it is not what you hoped to hear. You will always know where you stand, what the risks are, and what your options really are — no sugar-coating, no hidden agendas.

Integrity

The highest ethical standards in every matter, no exceptions. We act in your best interests, keep your confidences, and uphold the duties of the profession — before profit, before convenience, always.

Commitment

The time, expertise and care needed to reach the outcome you need. From first enquiry to final resolution, we stay with your matter, answer your questions, and see it through to the end.
